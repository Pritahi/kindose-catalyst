# Judge walkthrough

1. Open the app and explore the fictional sample circle. Notice the sample badge.
2. Check a dose in as taken. The remaining-dose count changes. Undo it, or mark a dose skipped with a note.
3. Add/edit a medication and an appointment. Never use real prescription information.
4. Switch to family or care-partner view. Ordinary collaboration remains available, but elder-owned profile, membership and consent controls are restricted.
5. Return to elder view, add a preference and generate a care handoff. It is a factual coordination summary, not clinical advice.
6. Open reminders. Compare planned/preview/failed/sent status honestly. Sample circles do not send mail.
7. To test authentication, create a new fictional circle, save its ID, invite a member with a unique passphrase, sign out and join as that member. Revoke them as the elder and confirm their next request is rejected.
8. Try a 390px-wide viewport and senior reading mode. Keyboard users can use the skip link, focus indicators and dialogs.

The proactive sender can be tested only with an approved recipient and configured provider. Do not claim the sample screen itself proves an email was delivered.
